100 Revere Street

Brand-new, never-lived-in apartments in Revere, Massachusetts, minutes from Revere Beach and a short Blue Line ride from downtown Boston.

About the Property

100 Revere Street is a newly constructed 32-unit residential building in Revere, MA. Every apartment is brand new, and the building includes elevator access and an included parking spot for each unit, features that are uncommon at comparable price points in the Greater Boston rental market.

The property is professionally managed by Green Ocean Property Management, a locally based, highly rated management company serving Greater Boston.

Amenities

Availability

The building offers a mix of one- and two-bedroom floor plans across five floors. Available units are generally priced in roughly the $2,500 to $2,900 per month range, with corner units and higher floors (some with Boston skyline views) toward the upper end. Availability changes frequently as units lease.

Neighborhood

Revere Beach, one of the country's oldest public beaches, is just minutes away, and the MBTA Blue Line offers a direct connection to downtown Boston and Logan Airport in about 25 minutes. The surrounding area has a growing food scene and significant ongoing waterfront development.
